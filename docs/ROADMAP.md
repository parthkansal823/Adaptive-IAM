# Roadmap

## Implemented research milestone

Adaptive authentication and owner/quorum authorization; timed grants/revocation; registered OIDC websites with Node/Python permission SDKs; verified passkeys/TOTP/recovery; private first-factor onboarding; request-observed session gating and source-session-linked website authority; organization app onboarding/rotation/callback diagnostics; 12-scenario read-only Attack Lab; responsive dark/light console; Docker isolation; local Ethereum registry and optional four-validator Besu QBFT with isolated signer and measured crash/outage recovery.

Research includes five full retraining runs with matched validation controls/reliability bins, an independently sourced 200,000-row synthesized RBA prefix with temporal/user-disjoint proxy studies, 18 multilingual author diagnostics, blind human annotation/adjudication workflow, measured reports, figures, readings and a manuscript. The [uncertainty extension](UNCERTAINTY_STUDY.md) adds five fresh fits and four distribution/telemetry stress conditions with conformal sets, held-out detection/friction costs and class coverage failures. Regressions and ineligible studies remain visible. Original assets remain preserved.

Operational additions include encrypted same-host Node website sessions, [validated offline IAM backup/restore](BACKUP.md), request/grant search and expiry-aware displays, and portable checks covering browser fixtures and an ephemeral live EVM/API integration. These controls do not certify a production identity provider.

## Evidence requiring independent collection

Real organizational outcomes and trusted telemetry; representative rare takeover labels; at least two independent human language annotators and separate adjudication; physical-device passkey usability; independently configured BERT measurements; subgroup/fairness and privacy/retention studies. Human access votes must never substitute for attack truth. Improve deployment models only from prespecified validation, then disclose untouched test performance.

## Deployment and interoperability

OIDC conformance/security review; invitation-based/private tenant directory and resource ownership; complete tenant isolation; multi-host session availability; HTTPS/managed secrets and factor recovery; versioned database migrations, encrypted off-host disaster recovery and retention operations; stronger trusted device signals; independent validator operators, Byzantine fault testing and HSM signer. Local Docker demonstrations and synthesized scores do not certify these properties.
