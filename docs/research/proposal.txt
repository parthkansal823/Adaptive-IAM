Project Title: - 
A Decentralized Identity and Access Management Framework Using Blockchain and Large Language Models

Project Scope: - (Max 500 words)
In digital world, managing access to authorized person is the most critical challenges in cybersecurity. Traditionally, whole identity access system heavily relies on centralized identity provider. This is an static authentication mechanism which are increasing vulnerable to data breaches, insider threats and cyberattacks.Identity and Access Management (IAM) is the framework responsible for ensuring the right individuals or systems access the right resources at the right time. However, modern IAM systems struggle with issues such as centralized trust, poor scalability, identity theft, and lack of intelligent decision-making.To overcome these limitations, we planned to emerge modern technologies like blockchain and large language models (LLMs) into IAM’s. 
Why Blockchain and LLMs?Language Models (LLMs) are being explored. Blockchain introduces decentralization and immutability, while LLMs bring intelligence, context-awareness, and adaptive decision-making. The integration of IAM with Blockchain and LLMs represents a next-generation access control paradigm. Thus, combining these three will provides trust, intelligence and control.
Research Objectives
Design a decentralized IAM architecture using blockchain
Integrate LLMs for intelligent access decisions
Enable dynamic, context-aware authorization
Improve resistance against insider threats
Maintain auditability and compliance
